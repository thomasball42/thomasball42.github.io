gdrivesync

A personal tool for syncing files between my computer and my Google Drive.

What it does

gdrivesync is a private Google OAuth client used by its owner with rclone, an open-source file sync program running on the owner's own computer. It lets rclone sign in to the owner's Google account to upload, download, and sync files with Google Drive.

Google data it uses

The app requests access to:

  • Google Drive: view, create, edit, and delete files, to keep local folders and Drive in sync

Files are transferred only between the owner's computer and their own Google Drive. No data is sent to any other server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who can use it

The app is intended for use by its owner only and is not offered as a public service.
